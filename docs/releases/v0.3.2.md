# CiruStrixLink 0.3.2

Version 0.3.2 detects the FastFlowLM NPU service as a competing model workload
and corrects the isolation record for the 256K DFlash campaign.

## Competing-workload detection

- Launch checks both `qwen-main.service` and `flm-npu.service` on each host.
- A loaded GLM rank with another model active is shown as `loaded · shared`,
  with the exact competing service named beside the host.
- A new paired load is refused while either competing service is active, and
  the blocker identifies the host and service that must be stopped.
- CiruStrixLink reports the conflict; it does not stop, mask, disable, replace,
  or otherwise change either service.

## Corrected 256K evidence

FastFlowLM's `flm-npu.service` was active on Ciru throughout the original
target-only, k=3, k=5, and HumanEval measurements published with 0.3.1. Its
lifetime memory peak was 11.5 GiB. Those speed rows are now labeled
mixed-workload evidence. HumanEval's 10/10 pass@1 result remains a valid
correctness gate.

After the NPU service was stopped, two identical repetitions of the exact
65,680-token k=5 request measured:

| Repetition | TTFT | Output-only TG | Draft acceptance | Mean accepted length |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| NPU-off 1 | 174.79 s | 12.93 tok/s | 29.14% | 2.46 |
| NPU-off 2 | 174.91 s | 14.56 tok/s | 34.26% | 2.71 |
| Pooled / mean | 174.85 s | 13.70 tok/s | — | — |

Both repetitions clear the 10 generated tokens/s operating requirement. k=5
therefore remains the provisional loaded setting. A fully isolated comparison
against k=3 and target-only is deferred until a maintenance window because it
requires unloading and reloading the active model.

## Validation constraints

- The GLM pair was not unloaded or restarted for the NPU-off confirmation.
- Prefix caching remained disabled.
- TP2, PP1, the 262,272-token profile, and the direct USB4 NHI path remained
  unchanged.
- The release tests validate the new NPU status and paired-load blocker.
